Our answer · Step 1

Stop it before it reaches the sea.

We take fresh slurry at the plant outlet, before discharge. This is the step that actually reduces what enters the gulf.

Agreement with GCT and the state needed

Scope: solid phosphogypsum only. The gas emissions behind the 2025 protests are a separate problem, and we do not claim to fix them.

Our answer · Step 2

Turn loose waste into a sealed block.

  1. 1
    Dewater and measureFilter presses make solid cake. Every batch is tested for radium and metals by gamma spectrometry.
  2. 2
    StabilizeA binder locks contaminants into a solid and cuts leaching and radon release.
  3. 3
    EncapsulateAn HDPE liner, then a marine-grade concrete shell. Cured, tested and certified before it ships.

Three barriers: binder, liner, shell. No single failure causes a release.

The road to the first turbine

Five gates between here and the horizon.

Months 0–12

Lab and mesocosm

Recipes, leach, radon and seawater-aging tests. Gate: results published, pass criteria met.

Months 9–24

Land test blocks

Sealed modules with sensors on a controlled site. Gate: a clean year of data.

Months 24–42

Nearshore pilot

Anchor or breakwater blocks in shallow water, fishers present. Gate: regulator sign-off.

Months 36–60

Flagship turbine

Gravity-base foundation with a maker and a developer. Gate: novel-material certification.

Year 5 onward

Scale and diversify

Several foundations, breakwaters and anchor blocks running together.

Earning trust

We measure. Everyone can see.

  • Independent measurementA third-party lab tests every batch. Results go on a public dashboard.
  • Fishers at the tableA community board of fishers and residents reviews monitoring and can trigger inspections.
  • A removal guaranteeIf monitoring fails, we retrieve the modules, funded by a reserve held outside the company.
  • No cover for pollutionThis complements ending the discharge. It does not replace it.
